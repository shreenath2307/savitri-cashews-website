Tree to jar

Eight steps. Keep scrolling.

1

Harvest

Ripe cashew apples are hand-picked from orchards and the raw nut is gently twisted off the fruit.

2

Sun dry

Raw nuts are dried in open yards under the sun to reduce natural moisture and stabilize the nut.

3

Boil

Raw cashews are boiled under controlled heat to soften the tough outer shell and prepare them for clean cracking.

4

Shell separating

The softened shell is carefully cracked and separated to release the intact whole cashew kernel inside without breakage.

5

Oven dry

Kernels go into temperature-controlled hot-air drying ovens (Borma) to loosen the inner skin and seal in crispness.

6

Peel

The delicate reddish-brown inner skin (testa) is removed cleanly, uncovering the pure, creamy ivory kernel.

7

Grade

Every kernel is sorted by size, colour, and shape into whole grades (W180, W210, W240, W320, W400) and splits.

8

Pack

Weighed with precision and sealed into airtight pouches, jars, and cartons to lock in freshness and rich crunch.

Cashew grades

Know your W-number.

The number is roughly how many whole kernels weigh one pound. Lower means bigger.

In your kitchen

Three ways to use a jar.

Simple ideas from home cooks. Pure, freshly graded cashews do the heavy lifting.

15 min · Velvety Base

Cashew cream & curries

A silky, dairy-free base that lends rich body and restaurant-style creaminess to gravies, pasta, and soups.

  • 1 cup Savitri whole cashews, soaked in warm water for 2 hours
  • ½ cup fresh water, pinch of sea salt
  • Blend until silky smooth. Stir into shahi gravies, korma, or creamy pasta sauces.
35 min · Festive Sweet

Traditional Kaju Katli

The timeless festival favourite, prepared with only three ingredients. Naturally sweet, buttery, and melts in the mouth.

  • 1 cup Savitri cashews, ground into a fine powder
  • ½ cup sugar and ¼ cup water simmered to one-string consistency
  • Knead to a soft dough, roll between sheets, and cut into iconic diamonds.
25 min · Fragrant Main

Royal Cashew Pulao

Aromatic basmati rice cooked with whole spices and topped with crisp, golden ghee-roasted cashews.

  • ½ cup Savitri cashews fried in pure ghee until crisp & golden
  • Cook aged basmati rice with bay leaf, cloves, and cardamom
  • Gently fold in the roasted cashews right before serving for maximum crunch.

Questions

Good to know.

How should I store cashews?

Keep them in an airtight jar in a cool, dry place. For more than two months, the fridge keeps them fresh longer.

Do you sell small packs and bulk?

Yes. Retail packs start at 250 g. For shops, caterers and gifting we supply bulk cartons at wholesale rates.

What is the difference between W180, W210, W240, W320 and W400?

They are grades of whole kernels by count per pound. Lower numbers mean bigger nuts: W180 and W210 are royal jumbo kernels, W240 and W320 are medium favourites, and W400 is smaller and ideal for everyday cooking and sweets.
